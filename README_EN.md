# Kikune

**An Android doujin voice library and audio player.**

Kikune is an Android app for organizing and listening to personal collections of doujin voice works, Japanese ASMR, and other audio content.

[한국어 README](README.md)

> **Development status:** Early development. Kikune is being adapted from [AniZen](https://github.com/salmanbappi/AniZen) into an audio-focused app for doujin voice collections. Features and architecture may change during development.

## Goals

- Organize local audio works in a personal library.
- Manage multiple tracks under a single work.
- Play audio and remember playback position.
- Support background playback and notification/media controls.
- Preserve and adapt useful library management, sorting, theming, and backup features from the upstream project.

## Scope

The initial development focus is **local audio file management and playback**.

The following are intentionally out of scope for now:
- DLsite integration
- Online work catalogs and external-site search
- Web scraping
- External sources and extensions

These features may be considered separately in the future.
